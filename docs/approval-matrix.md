# Approval Matrix (Production)

## Core Rules
1. Every claim must be project-linked and evidence-backed.
2. A claimant cannot approve their own claim.
3. CEO/CTO has final financial approval authority.
4. Claims without evidence stay in Queried until corrected.
5. Claims linked to unpaid client invoices can be approved but payment deferred.

## Amount Tiers
1. UGX 0 - 100,000
- Review: evidence and project-link check
- Final approval: CEO/CTO

2. UGX 100,001 - 500,000
- Review: COO/Finance evidence verification
- Final approval: CEO/CTO

3. Above UGX 500,000
- Review: COO/Finance and project verification
- Final approval: CEO/CTO + at least one other director acknowledgement

## Director/Executive Claim Rules
1. Director claim:
- Reviewed by another director or operations reviewer
- Final approval by CEO/CTO

2. CEO/CTO claim:
- Reviewed by COO or another director
- Final approval by another director (no self-approval)

## Decision States
1. Pending Review
2. Cleared for CEO/CFO Approval
3. Approved
4. Partially Approved
5. Queried
6. Rejected
7. Paid
